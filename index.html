Trading Agent Backup

Trading Agent Backup is a private, self-hosted utility for creating encrypted backups of a paper-trading simulation's local data to the account owner's Google Drive.

It uses rclone with its crypt remote to encrypt backup contents before uploading them to a dedicated Google Drive backup folder. The tool is not a trading platform and does not place trades.

Google Drive authorization is used solely for backup operations, such as uploading, listing, downloading, and restoring backup files. The owner controls the connected Google account and the server.
