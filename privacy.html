Privacy Policy

Trading Agent Backup · Last updated: November 18, 2026

Purpose

Trading Agent Backup is a private, self-hosted utility operated by the owner of the connected Google account. It uses Google Drive only to store, list, retrieve, and manage backups of local paper-trading simulation data.

Information processed

The utility processes Google OAuth credentials needed for authorization, backup file contents, file names and metadata, and operational logs. It does not request Google account data for advertising or profiling. The permissions displayed on Google's consent screen may permit broader Drive access than the dedicated backup folder; the utility is intended to operate only on that folder.

How information is used and protected

Backups are encrypted using rclone crypt before upload to Google Drive. OAuth credentials and encryption configuration are stored privately on the owner's server and are not published in this website or its GitHub repository. Google Drive provides remote storage, and the owner's hosting provider runs the backup process.

Sharing

Information is not sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing. Google Drive and the server hosting provider process data as needed to provide storage and hosting.

Retention and deletion

Backup files remain in the owner's Google Drive until deleted by the owner or by configured backup-retention rules. The owner can delete backup files from Google Drive and revoke OAuth access through their Google Account security settings. Local credentials and logs are managed on the owner's server.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the app owner using the support email shown on the Google OAuth consent screen. Do not post credentials or private data in public GitHub issues.

This independent personal utility is not affiliated with Google or eToro.
